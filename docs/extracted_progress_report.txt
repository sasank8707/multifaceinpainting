1. Executive Summary & Core Research Concept
Facial inpainting under severe occlusion (≥ 20% to 100% facial surface coverage) presents a fundamental challenge for generative
vision models. Standard text-to-image inpainting pipelines (e.g., vanilla Stable Diffusion) rely exclusively on unoccluded boundary pixels
and text prompts; consequently, when critical biometrics such as the nose, mouth, or eyes are missing, the model synthesizes a
plausible facial structure that does not correspond to the subject's true biometric identity.
Proposed Architectural Solution: We implement a three-stage multi-reference framework consisting of (1) Mask-Aware Reference
Retrieval & Scoring (O1), (2) Decoupled Identity Cross-Attention with Regional Transformer Fusion (O2), and (3) Verifier-Aware
Sampling with Quality-Constrained Reranking (O3). The architecture is designed to decouple WHERE facial organs should go
(geometry from target mask and landmarks) from WHO the subject is (dense ArcFace spatial tokens sourced 100% from verified
external reference photos). This structural separation prevents target identity leakage into the cross-attention values while enforcing
bit-exact preservation on unmasked target pixels.

2. Mathematical Formulations & Data Flow
Decoupled Identity Cross-Attention: To prevent text conditioning interference, U-Net cross-attention layers execute parallel dual-path
processing:
    Z_out = Softmax(Q K_text^T / √d) V_text + λ · Softmax(Q K_id^T / √d) V_id
where K_id = W_k T_fused and V_id = W_v T_fused are trained projection layers initialized at zero. Under Classifier-Free Guidance
(CFG), identity tokens are duplicated across both unconditional and conditional batches so identity steering operates strictly at
calibrated scale λ = 0.10 rather than being multiplied by guidance scale.
Invariance & Anti-Copy Guarantee: The diffusion backbone operates strictly on masked inputs where occluded pixels are zeroed out:
I_masked = I_target ■ (1 - M). The final reconstruction composites the decoded latent output with the original target canvas: I_final = (1
- M) ■ I_target + M ■ I_restored. This mathematically ensures exact pixel preservation (∆ = 0.0) on all unmasked pixels while barring
direct copy-paste from within the mask.
