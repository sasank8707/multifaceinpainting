Research & Technical Details

Architecture & Implementation Details

  • Mode 1 (Face Inpainting & Restoration): Driven by a Stable Diffusion 1.5 inpainting backbone conditioned on ArcFace identity embeddings via a frozen Step 150 Decoupled Identity Cross-Attention adapter. Includes O1 Mask-Aware Reference Quality Scoring and O2 Regional Identity Token Fusion across up to 3 references.
  • Automatic Damage Detection: LocalizedDamageDetector_v2 combining LAB color anomaly mapping and multi-scale texture deviations with facial landmark shielding for orbital and lip preservation.
  • Boundary Harmonization & Invariance: Inward feathering ($r = 7\text{ px}$) composited strictly inside the damage mask, guaranteeing mathematical invariance ($\Delta_{\max} = 0.0$, 0 changed pixels) on unmasked canvas pixels.
  • Known Limitation: Deep interior microtexture of large synthesized damage patches exhibits smoother synthetic skin (documented baseline limitation, preserved without ad-hoc sharpening).
  • Mode 2 (Background Removal): Pretrained LRASPP MobileNetV3-Large segmentation backbone generating transparent RGBA PNG cutouts with bit-exact original RGB pixels.
  • Mode 3 (Deblurring): Multi-scale edge-preserving bilateral detail decomposition with adaptive gradient sharpening and contrast enhancement.
